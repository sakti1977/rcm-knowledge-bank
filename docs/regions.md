# Regional challenges and controls

Profiles cover named programs and hospital settings. Each case uses fictional activity for an elective episode. Compare the evidence required to resolve a payment difference, alongside any patient impact.

| Profile | Documented challenge | Practical control and metric | Synthetic exercise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| U.S. Medicare and a separate commercial illustration | HHS OIG found that 13% of sampled denied Medicare Advantage authorization requests met Medicare coverage rules in its 2022 report. The 2024 Change Healthcare disruption delayed claims or payments for affected providers. | Track authorization decisions and supporting evidence. Use a durable delivery ID, contingency routes, and an unknown-delivery worklist. Monitor authorization turnaround and acknowledgment gaps by payer. | Verify a commercial payment shortfall using an effective contract and 835. Separately test a missing receipt in the claims-platform simulator. |
| India, AB PM-JAY | The 2023 national CAG audit identified preauthorization, settlement and validation problems. A Bihar audit reported delay for 75% of in-state claims and 54% of interstate claims in the respective groups it analyzed. These are historical audit groups with distinct denominators. | Version package and state rules, tie evidence to the authorization, and track provider response, adjudication and disbursement as separate stages. Track queries and age by stage, including pending items. | A queried package claim waits 4 days for the provider response, 5 for adjudication and 10 for disbursement. Explain who owns each part of the 19-day interval and verify whether the patient was improperly asked to pay. |
| England, NHS-funded secondary care | The 2026/27 NHS Payment Scheme uses several payment mechanisms. An activity discrepancy has different implications under a fixed component and under activity-linked terms. | Record service scope, payment mechanism, reporting vintage and adjustment terms. Track activity completeness and the value of unresolved reconciliations. | A synthetic elective reconciliation priced 100 eligible episodes; 104 are validated after cutoff. Determine whether and when the four late records affect payment under the agreed terms. |
| Australia, public hospitals | Classification and weighting affect activity-based funding. The 2026–27 determination uses AR-DRG v12.0 for admitted acute pricing. Small rural hospital services may be block funded under a separate cost determination. | Retain grouper and pricing versions, monitor regrouping variance and reconcile actual weighted activity against estimates. Track rural funding adjustments under the relevant arrangement. | Estimated weighted activity is 120 synthetic units and validated actual activity is 126. Investigate the six-unit difference before calculating the funding impact. Compare with a block-funded case. |

## Incentives and patient impact

Pair metrics with a balancing measure. For authorization, measure patient delay alongside documentation completeness. For collections, measure corrected bills and unresolved patient complaints alongside recovery. For activity-based funding, audit documented case mix alongside the change in weighted activity. For block-funded services, monitor access and service coverage alongside expenditure. Compare measures only after aligning scope and definitions. The [WHO–World Bank global UHC report](https://www.who.int/teams/health-financing-and-economics/global-monitoring-report/2025) provides context on financial protection.
